# MyDojo Pro Shop Karate Uniform Reference

The official beginner uniform is the **Kihon Gi** shown in the MyDojo pro shop. The reference image has been saved at `/home/ubuntu/webdev-static-assets/mydojo-kihon-gi-reference.png` for visual generation only.

The Karate silhouette uses a crisp plain-white wrap-over gi jacket with a **white V-neck lapel**, straight sleeves, loose white pants, and a white belt. It does **not** use the black-and-red V-neck trim seen in Taekwondo doboks. The MyDojo mark is a restrained vertical gray `MYDOJO` wordmark on the left chest, paired with a small red-and-black circular kicking-figure emblem above it; the pants repeat the vertical mark near the lower leg. A small black rectangular uniform label appears low on the jacket hem.

Carousel generations for beginner and kids programs must use this traditional Karate gi silhouette, a white belt for new students, and no colored V-neck trim.
