# Family Enrollment UI Verification

The public `/enroll?package=1` information step was reviewed in the development preview. It displays an **Enrolling more family members?** section with an **Add member** control and explains that second and third members receive 50% off recurring monthly tuition.

Selecting **Add member** reveals a Family Member 2 name field, a membership-plan selector offering Foundation, Black Belt, and Plus, and a remove control. The primary enrollment information remains available above the family section.
