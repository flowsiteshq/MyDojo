# Account Agreement Verification

The authenticated member dashboard was opened and the Account tab was selected from the persistent bottom navigation. The next review step is to confirm the new Documents card opens the signed enrollment agreement in its read-only dialog.

## Documents and agreement dialog

The Account dashboard shows a **Documents** card with a **Signed Enrollment Agreement** entry. Selecting it opens a scrollable, read-only agreement dialog with the plan summary, agreement sections, electronic-signature record, and a clear Close control. The preview enrollment used for verification is a legacy record without stored signature metadata; the dialog now states that accurately, while signed records display the saved name and signing date.
